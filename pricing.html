Quick answers

Do I need an account?

Not for Free. For Pro you get a license key by email that you paste into Clipwise’s settings. Your invoices, plan and key are in your customer portal, which you open with just your email address.

Does Pro send my pages anywhere?

No. Clipwise still talks only to Notion. The license key is checked with Lemon Squeezy now and then; your pages, highlights and Notion secret never go there.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in the customer portal. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and everything free keeps working after that.

What does lifetime mean?

Pay once, and Pro is yours for as long as Clipwise exists, including new Pro features.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, one license works in up to 5 browsers.
