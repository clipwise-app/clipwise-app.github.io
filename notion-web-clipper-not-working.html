Notion Web Clipper not working? Seven fixes that actually help

The clipper saves nothing, saves only a link, or drops half the article. Most of the time it's one of a handful of causes, and most of them take a minute to fix. Here they are, in the order worth trying — and what to do when the problem is the clipper itself.

Jump to your problem

  1. It doesn't save anything, or asks you to log in
  2. It saves to the wrong account or workspace
  3. It stopped working after a browser or privacy change
  4. Your database isn't in the "Add to" list
  5. It saves only a link, or half the page
  6. You can't set tags or properties while clipping
  7. Nothing above worked
1

Log into notion.so in the same browser

symptom: nothing saves, a spinner that never finishes, or a login prompt

The official clipper doesn't have its own login. It borrows the Notion session in your browser, so if you're logged out of notion.so in that browser — or only use the Notion desktop app — it has nothing to save with.

  1. Open notion.so in a new tab of the same browser and log in.
  2. Go back to the page you wanted to clip and reload it.
  3. Click the clipper again.
2

Check which account and workspace it's using

symptom: the clip "works", but it isn't where you expected

If you use more than one Notion account — say a work and a personal one — the clipper saves to whichever you're logged into in the browser. Log out of notion.so, log back in with the account you actually want, and then pick the right workspace in the clipper's menu.

3

Let notion.so keep its cookies

symptom: it used to work, then stopped after a browser update or a new extension

Because the clipper relies on your Notion login, anything that clears or blocks Notion's cookies can break it: strict privacy settings, "clear cookies on exit", or ad and tracker blockers.

  • In your browser's cookie or privacy settings, add notion.so to the sites that are always allowed to use cookies, and make sure it isn't cleared when you close the browser.
  • Temporarily switch off ad or privacy blockers on the page you're clipping, and try again.
  • Restart the browser after changing these settings.
4

Search for your database instead of scrolling

symptom: your reading list or database isn't in the list

The "Add to" menu doesn't always list everything. Type part of the database's name into its search box, and check you're in the right workspace (see fix 2). If you just want somewhere to save, the same menu can create a new links database for you.

5

If it saves only a link or half the article

symptom: a page with a title and a few lines, or just a bookmark

This one is the clipper, not you. Notion's own help page says content can go missing depending on how a website is built, and there's no setting that fixes it. Two things sometimes help:

  • Scroll through the whole article first. Many sites only load text and images as you scroll, and the clipper saves what's there at that moment.
  • Try again after the page has fully loaded, especially on news sites with heavy scripts.
Still half empty? Sites built from web components, old table layouts or heavy scripts often come through wrong no matter what you do. A clipper that finds the article itself, instead of copying the page as-is, is the real fix — see below.
6

Setting tags and properties while you clip

symptom: every clip lands as a bare row you still have to fill in

You're not missing a button: Notion's help page says tags can't be added while clipping. You have to open the saved page in Notion and fill them in afterwards. If that's the part that bothers you, it's a limit of the official clipper rather than something to troubleshoot.

7

Reinstall, and rule out other extensions

symptom: none of the above changed anything

  1. Remove the Notion Web Clipper from chrome://extensions, restart Chrome, and install it again.
  2. Open a guest window or a fresh Chrome profile, install only the clipper, log into notion.so and try one page. If it works there, another extension in your normal profile is getting in the way.
  3. If it fails even there, it's a problem on Notion's side — tell their support which page you tried.

Quick answers

Why is my Notion Web Clipper not saving anything?

It uses the Notion login in your browser. Logged out of notion.so, logged into another account, or cookies for notion.so blocked — any of those stops it. Log in on notion.so in the same browser, allow its cookies, and reload the page.

Why does it only save a link or part of the page?

Content can go missing depending on how a site is built, and there's no setting to change that. Scrolling through the article first helps on lazy-loading sites; otherwise use a clipper that extracts the article itself.

Can I add tags while clipping?

Not with the official clipper — you add them in Notion afterwards. Clipwise lets you set tags, status and other properties in the popup before saving.

Is there a Notion web clipper without a login?

Clipwise has no account and no login of its own. You create an integration key in Notion once, paste it into the extension, and it talks straight to Notion's API from your browser. Nothing goes through a server in between.
