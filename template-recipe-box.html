About this template

Clip a recipe and keep the recipe, not the ads. Sort by meal and cuisine, plan what to cook this week, and keep your favourites one click away.

Views

  • Want to try — a board from “Want to try” to “Favorite”
  • By meal — breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert and snacks
  • All recipes — everything you saved, with tags like Quick and Vegetarian

How to use it

  1. Open the template link from your Pro email and click Duplicate in Notion.
  2. Install Clipwise (free, no account) and share this Recipe box with it during setup.
  3. Save any page with one click. The columns fill themselves in.
