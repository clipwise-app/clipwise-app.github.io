Setup guide

Connect Clipwise to Notion in about two minutes

Clipwise has no account, so instead of signing in you hand it a key that Notion gives you. That key decides exactly which databases it may write to — and you can take it away again at any time. Here's every step, including the two places people usually get stuck.

1

Install the extension

Clipwise runs on Chrome and on browsers built on it: Edge, Brave, Arc and Opera. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, and a setup guide opens by itself in a new tab.

2

Create your Notion integration

An integration is Notion's way of letting a tool write into your workspace without a middleman. Making one is free and takes about thirty seconds.

  1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations and click New integration.
  2. Give it a name — Clipwise works fine. Nobody else sees this name.
  3. Pick the workspace you clip into, and leave the type on Internal.
  4. Click Save, then open the integration and copy the Internal Integration Secret.

Choose Internal, not Public. Public integrations are for apps that other people log into, they need a review by Notion, and Clipwise can't use one. If the form starts asking you for a website and a privacy policy, you picked Public — go back and switch.
3

Paste the secret into Clipwise

Open the setup guide that appeared after installing, or click the Clipwise icon and go to its settings. Paste the secret in the field and save.

That secret is stored in your own browser, in chrome.storage.local. It is sent to one place only: Notion's own API. There is no Clipwise server it could travel through, because there isn't one.

Lost the secret? Notion shows it again on the integration's page — open notion.so/my-integrations, click your integration and reveal it there. If you'd rather start fresh, make a new integration and paste the new secret over the old one.
4

Connect your database to the integration

This is the step almost everyone misses. Creating the integration doesn't give it access to anything yet — you decide per database. Until you do this, Clipwise's database list stays empty, which looks like a bug but isn't.

  1. Open the database you want to clip into, as a full page rather than as a small table inside another page.
  2. Click the ··· menu at the top right of that page.
  3. Choose Connections, then Connect to, and pick your integration.
  4. Confirm. Notion asks once whether the integration may access this page.

It has to be a database, not an ordinary page. Clipwise saves a page as a new row, which only exists in a database — a table, board, list, calendar or gallery. If you connected a plain page, the list in Clipwise stays empty. Clipping into several databases? Repeat this for each one. Sharing a parent page shares everything inside it too.
5

Clip your first page

Open any article and click the Clipwise icon. Your database is in the list, the fields that the page tells us about are already filled in, and you pick a status or tags yourself. Click Save to Notion and you get a link straight to the new page.

  • The next clip is quicker: your last database and your usual values are remembered.
  • For pages you just want kept, press Ctrl+Shift+S (Cmd+Shift+S on a Mac). It saves to your last database without opening anything.
  • Open an article you've already clipped and Clipwise links you to the page you have, instead of making a second one.
Change the shortcut: go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts and set whatever you like. Useful if another extension already claimed that combination.

Something's not working

My database isn't in the list

Nine times out of ten the database isn't connected to the integration yet — go back to step 4. Notion only shows a tool what you explicitly hand it.

Already done that? Two more things to check: it must be a real database rather than a plain page, and it must live in the same workspace you selected when creating the integration. Switching workspaces means making a second integration for that one.

It says the token is invalid or unauthorized

Usually a copying slip: a missing character, or a space at the end. Copy the secret again from notion.so/my-integrations and paste it fresh.

If it still refuses, the integration may have been deleted or its secret regenerated in Notion. Make a new integration, paste the new secret and connect your database to it again.

The clipped page is empty or half missing

Some pages only load their article once you scroll, and some are built entirely out of images, which leaves nothing to save. Scroll through the article once before clipping and try again — that fixes most cases.

Still wrong? Send me the address of that page. Genuinely: most of the improvements in version 0.2 started as exactly that kind of message.

The keyboard shortcut does nothing

Chrome hands each shortcut to one extension only, so another one may have claimed it first. Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts to see who has it and set a different combination for Clipwise.

The shortcut also needs a database to have been used at least once, since it saves to your last one.

I don't see the Clipwise icon

It's hiding in the puzzle-piece menu at the top right of your browser. Open that menu and click the pin next to Clipwise to keep it on the toolbar.

How do I take access away again?

In Notion, open the database, choose ··· › Connections and remove the integration. Or delete the whole integration at notion.so/my-integrations, which cuts off every database at once.

Uninstalling the extension removes the secret from your browser along with it. Nothing about you is stored anywhere else, because there's nowhere else.
