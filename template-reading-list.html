About this template

Save an article with Clipwise and the title, source, author, dates, a summary and your highlights land in the right columns on their own. Move it from “To read” to “Done” as you go.

Views

  • To read — a board by status: To read, Reading, Done
  • All — every article you saved
  • Highlights — only the articles where you marked something

How to use it

  1. Get the free template and click Duplicate in Notion.
  2. Install Clipwise (free, no account) and share this Reading list with it during setup.
  3. Save any page with one click. The columns fill themselves in.
