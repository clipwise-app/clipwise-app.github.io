About this template

Save readings and videos while you study. Clipwise fills in the source, a summary and your highlights, sorted by course and week, so revising is reading one table instead of finding everything again.

Views

  • By course — a board with a column for every course
  • All — every reading and video, with week and status
  • Highlights — just the lines you marked, ready for revision

How to use it

  1. Open the template link from your Pro email and click Duplicate in Notion.
  2. Install Clipwise (free, no account) and share this Course notes with it during setup.
  3. Save any page with one click. The columns fill themselves in.
