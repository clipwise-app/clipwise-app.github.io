About this template

Save a YouTube video and it lands here as a video you can play in Notion, with the channel filled in. Drag it from “To watch” to “Watched”, or browse your videos as cards.

Views

  • Queue — a board from “To watch” to “Watched”
  • All videos — every video you saved, with channel and date
  • Gallery — your videos as cards

Properties

How to use it

  1. Open the template link from your Pro email and click Duplicate in Notion.
  2. Install Clipwise (free, no account) and share this Watch later with it during setup.
  3. Save any page with one click. The columns fill themselves in.
