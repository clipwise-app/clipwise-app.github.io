Quick answers

Why no account?

An account means a server with your data on it, and a login that can break. Clipwise keeps your Notion secret and your clips on your own computer, and that’s not changing for Pro.

I lost my license key

Open the customer portal with the email address you bought with, or search your inbox for “Clipwise”. Still stuck? Email me.

I use the free version. Do I need to do anything?

No. Free keeps working as it does, with no account and no key.
