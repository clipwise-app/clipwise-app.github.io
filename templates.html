Quick answers

Do I need Clipwise to use the template?

No, it's a normal Notion database and you can fill it in by hand. With Clipwise it fills itself in when you save an article.

Is the template really free?

Yes. Duplicate it into your own Notion and it's yours. No email, no sign-up.

When is Pro coming?

There's no date. Clipwise is made by one person, and the free version comes first. If you want to hear when it's ready, or tell me what it should have, send me an email.
