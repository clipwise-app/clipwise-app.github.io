How to use it

  1. Click Get the free template. It opens in Notion.
  2. Click the Duplicate icon at the top right and pick your workspace.
  3. Install Clipwise (free, no account) and choose this Reading list in its setup.
  4. Save any article with one click. The columns fill themselves in.

Quick answers

Do I need Clipwise to use the template?

No, it's a normal Notion database and you can fill it in by hand. With Clipwise it fills itself in when you save an article.

Is the template really free?

Yes. Duplicate it into your own Notion and it's yours. No email, no sign-up.

I duplicated it. Now what?

Install Clipwise. Its setup guide opens by itself; at the step "Share a database", open your Reading list in Notion, click ··· → Connections and add your integration.
