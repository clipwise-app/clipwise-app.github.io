Save articles to Notion without making another account

Most Notion clippers ask you to sign up with them first, and then your pages travel through their server on the way to your workspace. There is a simpler way: connect the clipper straight to Notion with a key you create yourself. Here's how that works and how to set it up.

?

Why clippers usually want an account

A clipper needs permission to write into your Notion. Most get it by having you log in with their own service, which then holds your Notion connection on its server. That's convenient for them, but it means another login that can break, another company holding your data, and often a free plan with limits.

Notion also offers a direct route: an integration key. You create it in your own Notion settings, choose which databases it may use, and paste it into the extension. From then on the extension talks to Notion's API straight from your browser. No account, no server in between, and you can revoke the key in Notion at any time.

1

Create a Notion integration key

  1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations and click New integration.
  2. Give it a name (for example "Clipwise"), pick your workspace, and keep it as an internal integration.
  3. Copy the secret. It starts with ntn_.
2

Paste it into the clipper

Install Clipwise. Its setup guide opens by itself; paste the key there. It's stored in your browser's local extension storage and is only ever sent to Notion.

3

Share your database with the key

In Notion, open the database you want to clip into, click ··· at the top right, then Connections, and add your integration. It only sees what you share with it, so the rest of your workspace stays private.

Most people get stuck here. It has to be a database (a table, board, list or gallery), not an ordinary page. The setup guide has pictures of this step.
4

Clip

Open an article and click the Clipwise icon. Pick your database, set a status or tags, and save. The whole article lands in Notion with author, source and date filled in. Next time, Ctrl Shift S saves straight to your last database without opening anything.

What "no account" means in practice
  • No sign-up, no email address, no password to forget.
  • No server: the extension talks only to api.notion.com.
  • No analytics or tracking inside the extension.
  • No free-plan limit, because there's no plan. Clipping is free.

The trade-off: a two-minute setup with the key, and it runs on Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Opera, not Safari or mobile.

Quick answers

Can I save to Notion without signing up for a clipper account?

Yes. Clipwise uses a Notion integration key that you create yourself, so there is no separate account. The extension then saves pages straight to Notion's API from your browser.

Is a Notion integration key safe to use?

The key only has access to the databases you share with it, and you can revoke it in Notion at any time. In Clipwise it is stored locally in your browser and sent only to Notion.

Why is my database not showing up in the clipper?

The database has to be shared with your integration: open it in Notion, click ··· at the top right, choose Connections and add the integration. It also has to be a database, not a normal page.

Does the official Notion Web Clipper need an account?

It has no separate account, but it uses your Notion login in the browser. If you are logged out of notion.so or cookies are blocked, it cannot save.
